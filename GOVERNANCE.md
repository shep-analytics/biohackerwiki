# Biohacking Wiki Governance

## Mission

To create an open-source, community-driven knowledge base for biohacking, nootropics, supplements, and self-optimization—built on evidence-based information with clear quality standards.

---

## Contributor Tiers

| Tier | Permissions | Requirements |
|------|-------------|--------------|
| **Reader** | View all content | None |
| **Contributor** | Create drafts, suggest edits | Account + email verified |
| **Editor** | Publish content, approve drafts | 5+ approved contributions OR vouched by existing editor |
| **Moderator** | Lock articles, resolve disputes, manage users | Elected by editors or appointed by admins |
| **Admin** | Full platform access | Core team |

### Earning Trust
- New contributors start with draft-only permissions
- Consistent, high-quality contributions unlock editor status
- Credentialed experts (researchers, practitioners) may fast-track with verification

---

## Content Policies

### Quality Standards
1. **Cite your sources** — Claims require references (studies, reputable sources)
2. **Tiered evidence** — Flag content as:
   - 🟢 **Strong** — Multiple RCTs, meta-analyses
   - 🟡 **Preliminary** — Limited studies, animal research
   - 🔴 **Anecdotal** — User reports, theoretical
3. **Neutral point of view** — Present information, not advocacy
4. **Harm reduction** — Include safety info, contraindications, interactions

### Prohibited Content
- ❌ Medical advice (use "consult a doctor" disclaimers)
- ❌ Vendor/affiliate links in main articles
- ❌ Unsubstantiated claims without evidence labeling
- ❌ Promotional content disguised as information

### Conflict of Interest
- Disclose if you sell, work for, or profit from products you write about
- Editors may add disclosure notices to articles

---

## Moderation

### Article Protection
- High-stakes articles (dosing, interactions) may be **locked** to editor-only
- Recent vandalism targets get temporary protection

### Dispute Resolution
1. Discuss on article's comment thread
2. If unresolved → Moderator reviews
3. If still unresolved → Admin makes final call

### Enforcement
| Violation | Consequence |
|-----------|-------------|
| Minor (style issues) | Edit corrected, guidance given |
| Moderate (unsourced claims) | Edits reverted, warning |
| Serious (misinformation) | Temporary suspension |
| Severe (spam, vandalism) | Permanent ban |

---

## Amendments

This governance document can be amended by:
- Proposal from any editor
- Discussion period (7 days minimum)
- Majority approval from active editors + admin sign-off
